What makes this different

Most warning systems tell you a district is at risk. This tells you what to do about it.

24-hour outlook

Rainfall trends, soil moisture, and terrain history combine into a day-ahead risk score for every hillside, not just instrumented districts.

Under-1-hour nowcast

When rainfall spikes on ground that's already shifting and neighbours report cracks, the system escalates immediately.

A place to actually go

Every alert comes with a route to the nearest verified safe zone, avoiding roads neighbours have marked blocked.

How it works

From satellite to your phone, in four steps.

01

Data comes in

Free satellite rainfall and ground-movement data, refreshed continuously.

02

Risk is scored

A model trained on historical landslide records turns data into a live risk level.

03

Neighbours confirm

Citizen reports of cracks or movement sharpen the alert in real time.

04

You're notified

Push, SMS, or a call — with a route to safety attached.
